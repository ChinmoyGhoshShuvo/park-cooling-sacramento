**Title:** Do Parks Cool the Streets Around Them? Matched-Pixel Evidence from Sacramento, 2016–2025

**Summary:** I matched 30 m Landsat pixels near 790 Sacramento parks with comparable pixels farther away, imaged on the same summer day across 20 smoke-free scenes from 2016 to 2025. Neighbourhoods next to parks are about 0.6 °C cooler at 50–100 m, 0.2 °C cooler at 150–200 m and no cooler beyond 200 m, and a park's tree canopy, not its size, best predicts how much it cools its surroundings. The project is a reproducible R pipeline with balance diagnostics, a negative control, placebo parks and Rosenbaum bounds.
