**Title:** Do Parks Cool the Streets Around Them? Matched-Pixel Evidence from Sacramento, 2016–2025

**Summary:** A reproducible R pipeline matches 30 m Landsat pixels near Sacramento's urban parks with comparable pixels farther away, imaged on the same summer day, to measure how far a park's cooling reaches into the surrounding streets and which park features drive it. The design includes balance diagnostics, a negative control, placebo parks and Rosenbaum bounds. The work is unpublished; results will be shared after publication.
